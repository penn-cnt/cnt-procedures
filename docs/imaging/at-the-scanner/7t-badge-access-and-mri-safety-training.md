# 7T Badge Access & MRI Safety Training

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    For Stellar Chance 7T badge access: complete Level 1 MRI Safety training in Workday, submit a CAMRIS ticket for in-person orientation, and bring the completed non-patient MRI screening form; CAMRIS then grants access. Duplicates the 7T Research Scans page.

For 7T badge access

1. Need Level 1 MRI Safety training (in Workday):

[camris.atlassian.net](https://camris.atlassian.net/)

[wiki/spaces/OP/pages/199294977/How+to+Access+Level+1+MRI+Safety+Training+in+Workday](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://camris.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/199294977/How*to*Access*Level*1*MRI*Safety*Training*in*Workday__;KysrKysrKysr!!IBzWLUs!WvbiYj7_zGYxHg7BF4LVP0U_d4gJn98BXi-_7EsaqRpDHDXV8GwNOXVVHXfZ5vaPHoH7919TFqSi-7eNKmEyFYvNPb0c$)

In-person orientation with CAMRIS staff:

    1. Put in a ticket to request training:

[camris.atlassian.net](https://camris.atlassian.net/)


    1. [servicedesk/customer/portal/6/group/12/create/145](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://camris.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/6/group/12/create/145__;!!IBzWLUs!WvbiYj7_zGYxHg7BF4LVP0U_d4gJn98BXi-_7EsaqRpDHDXV8GwNOXVVHXfZ5vaPHoH7919TFqSi-7eNKmEyFTAlFiSw$)
    2. Complete this form:

[www.med.upenn.edu](https://www.med.upenn.edu/)


    1. [camris/assets/user-content/documents/non-patient-mri-screening-form-clickable-final.pdf](https://www.med.upenn.edu/camris/assets/user-content/documents/non-patient-mri-screening-form-clickable-final.pdf)(and bring to in person training)
2. A member of the CAMRIS team will respond to the ticket and will notify you of the next available training date. Once the online and site-specific trainings are complete, CAMRIS will grant badgeaccessto the appropriate MRI facility (stellar chance in this case)
