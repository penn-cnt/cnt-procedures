# Running the 7T

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    At the 7T scan, bring two consent copies, 7T C2, W9, metal screening and demographic form; fill the REDCap 7T MRI and consenting fields. For controls, trigger the control check to Joel after Gabby uploads to PennChart. Includes the 7T consent-note template.

**\*\*2 copies of 7T consent, 7T C2, W9 (if patient has not already completed one from 3T), metal screening, demographic form**
REDCap Documentation While at Scanner ([REDCap Documentation at the Scanner](redcap-documentation-at-the-scanner.md))

REDCap:
7T MRI field

*   date of last seizure on REDCap, note if there was a seizure in the past 24 hours
*   date of scan
*   where scan was saved/uploaded
*   who was at scan
Consenting and subject numbers

*   consent date
*   consent note
+As much presurgical data as availabled

**CONTROLS ONLY-**
Once the scan has been uploaded to the person's PennChart by Gabby, you need to trigger the control check to be sent to Joel
He determines if the control is actually considered to be healthy vs not
