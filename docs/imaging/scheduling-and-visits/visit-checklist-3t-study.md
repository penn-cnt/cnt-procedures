# Visit Checklist: 3T Study

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    Checklist for a 3T research scan: book CAMRIS under protocol 819126, pend the PennChart order for Kate, add to trackers, create the REDCap RID; bring ICF, W-9, demographic form, and C-2; tell techs to upload to Flywheel with protocol V5; afterward complete REDCap and reimburse.

**Scheduling (done in advance):** 3T Research Scans ([3T Research Scans: Scheduling & Billing](3t-research-scans-scheduling-and-billing.md))

- [ ] [CAMRIS](https://pathbio.med.upenn.edu/camris/dogfish/) slot reserved on HUP6 FNDBAMR2 under protocol **819126**
- [ ] PennChart order pended and sent to Kate for signature
- [ ] Order linked to research study (Research Exam diagnosis associated)
- [ ] Radiology emailed to schedule once order is signed
- [ ] Scan added to SCHEDULE TRACKERS ([Scan Scheduling Trackers (sEEG, 3T/fMRI, 7T)](../../operations/scheduling-trackers/scan-scheduling-trackers-seeg-3t-fmri-7t.md))
- [ ] Scan added to CNT Imaging Google Calendar
- [ ] Order # entered into CAMRIS
- [ ] Patient reminder sent: 48-hour email + 24-hour call (Gabby)
- [ ] REDCap profile created for participant (so they have an research ID - RID)
- [ ] Flywheel handle ready: **sub-RIDXXXX@davis:presurgicalEpilepsy**

**Forms to bring day-of:**

*   **3T Informed Consent Form (ICF, most recent version, 2 copies)**
    *   [https://upenn.app.box.com/file/1236821439308](https://upenn.app.box.com/file/1236821439308)
*   W-9 Form

    [📄 Penn W9 form.pdf](../../assets/imaging/visit-checklist-3t-study/01-penn-w9-form.pdf)

*   Demographic Form

    [📄 Scan Demographic Form (3) (2).pdf](../../assets/imaging/visit-checklist-3t-study/02-scan-demographic-form-3-2.pdf)

*   C-2 Human Subject Voucher

    [📄 Penn C2.pdf](../../assets/imaging/visit-checklist-3t-study/03-penn-c2.pdf)

**Day-of reminders:**

- [ ] Tell techs to upload to Flywheel using **protocol V5**
- [ ] Tell techs to push BOTH fMRI and 3T scans to Flywheel
- [ ] Check in and out of CAMRIS while at the scanner for billing purposes
- [ ] Note date of last seizure (flag if seizure in past 24 hours)
- [ ] Confirm patient fills out: ICF, W9, C2, demographic form
- [ ] Confirm every required signature and date field is fully completed by patient/guardian and study team. Consent Signing Checklist ([Consent Signing Checklist](../../operations/consenting/consent-signing-checklist.md))

**After the scan:**

- [ ]     Fill out [https://redcap.med.upenn.edu/redcap\_v17.2.3/index.php?pid=23568](https://redcap.med.upenn.edu/redcap_v17.2.3/index.php?pid=23568)
    - [ ] All Consent Notes ([Consent Note Templates](../../operations/consenting/consent-note-templates.md))
    - [ ] Surgical outcomes REDCap Project ([Surgical Outcomes REDCap Project](../../redcap/projects-and-data-entry/surgical-outcomes-redcap-project.md))
- [ ] Reimburse participant Reimbursing a Participant ([Reimbursing a Participant](../../operations/participant-reimbursement-greenphire/reimbursing-a-participant.md))
- [ ] Fill out as much presurgical info as available
- [ ] Contact Dr. Joel Stein for any controls Control Research Scans ([Control Scan Neuroradiologist Check](../at-the-scanner/control-scan-neuroradiologist-check.md))
