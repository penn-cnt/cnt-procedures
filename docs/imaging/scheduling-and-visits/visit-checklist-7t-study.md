# Visit Checklist: 7T Study

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    7T scan checklist: book a 2-hour CAMRIS slot under protocol 818407, confirm a physician will attend; bring ICF, W-9, C-2, demographic and MRI screening forms; meet patient in HUP6 lobby, patients get 2D CEST only, techs upload to Pedro. Seizure: call Penn Police (215) 898-7297.

**Scheduling (done in advance):**

- [ ] CAMRIS 2-hour slot booked under protocol **818407**
- [ ] Scan added to SCHEDULE TRACKERS ([Scan Scheduling Trackers (sEEG, 3T/fMRI, 7T)](../../operations/scheduling-trackers/scan-scheduling-trackers-seeg-3t-fmri-7t.md))
- [ ] Scan added to Imaging Calendar
- [ ] Physician confirmed to attend scan (required for patients)
- [ ]     Epilepsy fellows/providers emailed with updated 7T schedule
    *   [EpilepsyProv@uphs.upenn.edu](mailto:EpilepsyProv@uphs.upenn.edu); [EpilepsyFellows@pennmedicine.upenn.edu](mailto:EpilepsyFellows@pennmedicine.upenn.edu)
- [ ] Patient reminder sent (Gabby)
- [ ] Your Level 1 MRI Safety training is current (required for Stellar Chance access)

**Day-of reminders:**

- [ ] Meet patient in **HUP6 lobby** (3400 Spruce Street) and walk them to Stellar Chance
- [ ] Confirm physician is present before scanning a patient
- [ ] Patients get **2D CEST only**; controls get **2D and 3D CEST**
- [ ] Techs upload to **Pedro** (not Flywheel)
- [ ] Have clinician's phone number handy in case of seizure
- [ ] Check in and out of camris while at the scanner for billing purposes
- [ ] Confirm patient fills out: ICF, W9, C2, demographic form, metal screening form
- [ ] Confirm every required signature and date field is fully completed by patient/guardian and study team. Consent Signing Checklist ([Consent Signing Checklist](../../operations/consenting/consent-signing-checklist.md))

**After the scan:**

- [ ] Have Jacqui burn the CD (patient initials + birthday on label)
- [ ] Pick up CD next day and drop off to Gabby at PEC
- [ ]     Fill out [https://redcap.med.upenn.edu/redcap\_v17.2.3/index.php?pid=23568](https://redcap.med.upenn.edu/redcap_v17.2.3/index.php?pid=23568)
    - [ ] All Consent Notes ([Consent Note Templates](../../operations/consenting/consent-note-templates.md))
- [ ] Contact Dr. Joel Stein for any controls Control Research Scans ([Control Scan Neuroradiologist Check](../at-the-scanner/control-scan-neuroradiologist-check.md))

* * *

**Emergency (7T only):** If a patient has a seizure, remove from scanner, move to gurney, notify the clinician on-site, and call Penn Police at **(215) 898-7297** if ED transport is needed. Rapid Response does NOT respond to Stellar Chance.
