# Adding a Non-Penn / New Hire to a Study

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    Five-step checklist for adding a non-Penn collaborator or new hire to a Penn-IRB study: get PennKey and REDCap account, request HSERA access (ORG code 4666), submit an HSERA personnel modification with CITI certificate, add to REDCap projects/DAGs, then update the study personnel list.

Follow these steps when adding a non-Penn person to a study where Penn is the IRB of record or a new hire that needs the things below!

**Step 1: Follow these steps to get them a pennkey and REDCap account (if needed)**

*   REDCap Account Requests ([Requesting a REDCap Account](../access-and-accounts/requesting-a-redcap-account.md))

**Step 2: Request an HSERA account for them**

*   [https://upenn.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV\_1FVGNnePNAwraiW](https://upenn.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_1FVGNnePNAwraiW)
*   You will need their 8 digit Penn ID# and pennkey
*   For "4 digit ORG code" put **4666**

**Step 3: Create a modification in HSERA to add personnel**

*   The person you are adding will need to complete their CITI Biomedical Research-Human Research certificate in order to be added
*   [Instructions for accessing CITI Biomedical Research-Human Research course](https://upenn.app.box.com/file/1324538060987?s=bwjk5cr1d3b8r52dba0sc1pvkj5sl71s)

**Step 4: Adding to REDCap projects**

*   Once the IRB approves this personnel modification request, you can now add this person to the appropriate projects/DAGs in REDCap

**Step 5: Update the** study personnel list** (kept by the lab manager; location to be confirmed)
