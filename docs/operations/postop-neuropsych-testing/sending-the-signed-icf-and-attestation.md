# Sending the Signed ICF & Attestation

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    Two email templates for sending post-op neuropsych participants their signed informed consent form and signed attestation form, using the subject format 'Signed Copy - Informed Consent Form [protocol #]'.

**Signed ICF:**
Subject:
Signed Copy - Informed Consent Form **\[INSERT PROTOCOL NUMBER\]** (i.e. Signed Copy - Informed Consent Form 3T 819126)
Message:
Hi **\[INSERT PT NAME\]**,

Thank you for **re-consenting/consenting** to the research protocol for your post-operative neuropsychology testing. The signed copy of the consent form is attached to this email for your own records.

Best,
**\[INSERT YOUR NAME\]**
Penn Epilepsy Research Team

**Signed ICF:**
Subject:
Signed Attestation - Informed Consent Form **\[INSERT PROTOCOL NUMBER\]** (i.e. Signed Copy - Informed Consent Form 3T 819126)
Message:
Hi **\[INSERT PT NAME\]**,

Thank you for **re-consenting/consenting** to the research protocol for your post-operative neuropsychology testing. Attached below is a signed copy of the attestation form for your own records.

Best,
**\[INSERT YOUR NAME\]**
Penn Epilepsy Research Team
