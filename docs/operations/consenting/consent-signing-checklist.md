# Consent Signing Checklist

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    Checklist to run through before and during consent signing: correct ICF version, adequate review time, understanding check, verbal review of key elements, eligibility, required documents, private setting, complete signatures and dates, consent note, and upload per SOP.

**▢ Confirm Correct Consent Version**

*   ▢ Latest IRB‑approved version
*   ▢ Correct study title, PI, version number, version date
*   ▢ Correct consent type (7T, 3T, Pioneer, Collaborative, etc.)
**▢ Ensure Participant Had Adequate Review Time**

*   ▢ Provided consent form in advance (if applicable)
*   ▢ Participant had time to read without pressure
*   ▢ Participant had opportunity to ask questions
**▢ Verify Participant Understanding (Use Open‑Ended Questions)**

*   ▢ Participant can explain study purpose
*   ▢ Participant can describe procedures
*   ▢ Participant can identify risks
*   ▢ Participant understands voluntary nature and alternatives
**▢ Review Key Elements Verbally**

*   ▢ Purpose
*   ▢ Procedures
*   ▢ Risks & discomforts
*   ▢ Benefits
*   ▢ Confidentiality
*   ▢ Voluntary participation + right to withdraw
*   ▢ Compensation & costs
*   ▢ Contact information
**▢ Confirm Eligibility to Consent**

*   ▢ Participant is 18+ (unless assent/parental permission applies)
*   ▢ Participant is capable of informed decision‑making
*   ▢ No signs of coercion or impaired judgment
*   ▢ LAR documentation verified (if applicable)
**▢ Provide All Required Documents**

*   ▢ Full consent form
*   ▢ HIPAA authorization (if PHI collected)
*   ▢ Supplemental study‑specific documents
*   ▢ Copy for participant to keep
**▢ Prepare the Signing Environment**

*   ▢ Private, unrushed setting
*   ▢ Pen or electronic signature device ready
*   ▢ Participant reminded they can stop at any time
**▢ Complete Signature Section Correctly**

*   ▢ Participant signature + date
*   ▢ Person obtaining consent signature + date
*   ▢ Witness signature (if required)
*   ▢ All dates match and are legible
**▢ Document the Consent Encounter >>** _use this guide_ **>>** All Consent Notes ([Consent Note Templates](consent-note-templates.md))

*   ▢ Date/time consent obtained
*   ▢ Who obtained consent
*   ▢ Questions asked + clarifications provided
*   ▢ Participant received a copy
**▢ Store and Upload According to SOPs**

*   ▢ Upload signed consent to correct secure folder
*   ▢ Use required naming conventions
*   ▢ Maintain version control
*   ▢ Confirm HIPAA‑compliant storage
