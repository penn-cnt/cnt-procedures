# Roles

Every page lists the roles it serves in its front matter (`roles: [...]`). These are *reader roles*, not job titles: one person usually holds two or three. A clinical research coordinator follows `crc` and the REDCap pages tagged `data-rc`; data staff follow `data-rc` and `pipeline`; trainees follow `analyst`. Use the [Map](../map/index.md) in Role view, or the [Tags](../tags.md) page, to see what each role needs to know.

| Key | Role | Reads the pages about |
|---|---|---|
| `crc` | Clinical research coordinator / clinical RA | Participant-facing study conduct: visits, scanner day, consenting, testing, reimbursement, IRB paperwork. |
| `data-rc` | Data research coordinator / data RA | Data management: governance, standardization and integration, the data side of collection, analytics basics. |
| `pipeline` | Pipeline & systems maintainer | Keeps the processing chain, uploads, archives and servers running. |
| `analyst` | Trainee / analyst (postdoc, PhD, master's, undergraduate) | Runs analyses: compute environments, software, de-identified data access, the analysis pipelines. |
| `pi-manager` | PI / lab manager | Approvals, onboarding and offboarding, IRB, reporting, budgets, storage and PHI rules. |
| `collaborator` | External collaborator | Site partners: data sharing, standards, intake, contacts. |

Role assignments were drafted from each page's section on 2026-10-04 and are meant to be corrected as people use them: if a page is missing from your role's list, add the role to its front matter.
