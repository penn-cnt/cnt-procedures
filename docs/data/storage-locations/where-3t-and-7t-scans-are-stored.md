# Where 3T and 7T Scans Are Stored

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    Intro note: 3T scans are stored in Flywheel (tell the tech to upload there) and 7T scans in pedro; after any research scan, move the images into cnt-fs so other lab members can access them. Details are on the child pages.

### **Introduction:**
**3T SCANS ARE STORED IN FLYWHEEL (Tell tech to upload them here)**
**7T SCANS ARE STORED IN PEDRO**
After a 3T or 7T research scan, you need to move the scan into cnt-fs which is where the imaging will be stored so that other researchers in the lab can access the images for their projects. In this SOP, instructions are provided on how to move the images from a 3T or 7T scan into cnt-fs.
