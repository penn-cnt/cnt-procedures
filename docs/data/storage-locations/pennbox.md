# PennBox

!!! abstract "What this page tells you"
    PennBox requires a PennKey with a qualifying PennCommunity affiliation. Workaround for outside collaborators: they create a free personal box.com account and you share your PennBox folders with them. Written by Joshua Asuncion, 6/7/24.

### PennBox access without a Penn affiliation

> If you have a PennKey and one of the following affiliations in PennCommunity: Student, Faculty, Staff, Temporary Staff, Service Provider, Contingent Worker, Emeritus Retired Faculty/Staff, or HUP Corporate (HCOR), you are able to get access to PennBox.  If you do not have one of the above affiliations, you cannot get PennBox access.    
>   
> Here is a unofficial workaround:   
>   
> A user can register for a free account ([https://www.box.com/pricing/individual](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://www.box.com/pricing/individual__;!!IBzWLUs!VfeAUhxmVcNHnb40dUzW-wnVXdCrr7r4mxSf4qfcL17xb0QtG69PETI2Q73J6Mj3znANj6xoZdwGOSfSsisTmPPecIU$)) and then you can share access to your Penn Box folders with them. As far as I can tell as long as they are accessing files/folders created by PennBox user there are little to no restrictions.
